FILE 001

NKDT — Points

Can student housing be designed against loneliness?

Mixed-methods thesis combining a campus-wide survey, GIS density mapping and typology scoring. The findings are distilled into three buildable housing types — A, B and C — arranged as a connected masterplan of lab, community core and dorm clusters.

KEY FINDING

Clustered low-rise plexes outperform corridor dormitories on every measured belonging metric.
